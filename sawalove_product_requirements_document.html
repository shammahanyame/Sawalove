# SawaLove: Tinder for Cameroon - PRD ## Overview SawaLove is a dating and networking app specifically designed for the Cameroonian market. It combines the familiar swipe-based mechanics of Tinder with deep cultural localization (bilingual support, mobile money integration, and specific profile fields like 'Village of Origin'). ## Target Audience Cameroonians (both local and diaspora) looking for friendship, casual dating, marriage, or networking. ## Core Features - **Bilingual Support:** English & French. - **Swipe-to-Match:** Classic right for 'Like', left for 'Nope'. - **Localized Profiles:** Fields for Hometown, Region of Origin, Church/Denomination, and Languages Spoken. - **Verification:** Mandatory photo verification to build trust. - **Communication:** Chat, Voice Notes, and Video Calls. - **Monetization:** Premium tiers with MTN/Orange Mobile Money integration. - **Safety:** Block/Report, Incognito Mode, and Block Contacts. ## Screen List (MVP) 1. **Onboarding/Sign-up:** Phone number focus, bilingual toggle. 2. **Discovery (Swipe):** Profile cards with localized details. 3. **Explore:** Interest-based matching and 'Modes'. 4. **Matches & Likes:** Overview of mutual connections. 5. **Chat Detail:** Messaging with voice notes and safety features. 6. **User Profile (Self):** Settings, localized fields, and premium upsells. 7. **Peer Profile:** Detailed view of a potential match.
